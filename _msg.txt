feat(parser): named special-point meets — the NOUN form of a centre (#44)

Root cause: naming a concurrency point by its NOUN («X מפגש האלכסונים»/«התיכונים»
/«חוצי הזוויות»/«הגבהים»/«האנכים האמצעיים») was not-handled — only the explicitly-
lettered form («E חיתוך AC ו-BD») parsed. ~5-6 prod users named the diagonal
crossing this way; the operator asked to include it AND generalize to the four
triangle centres.

Fix (ADR-329, ADR-110 macro — no new engine construct): a `specialPointMeet` rule
over five CENTER_FAMILIES. Fires only on a meet cue (מפגש/נפגש/נחתכ/חיתוך/
intersection of/meet) AND a plural centre-noun stem, so the lettered forms stay
with their own rules. Shape resolves from a «במשולש ABC»/«במרובע ABCD» qualifier
else the figure's single polygon of the right size; ambiguity DEFERS (ADR-052).
Each lowers to two special lines + their existing intersection:
- diagonals → 2 segments + line-line-intersection (quad crossing)
- medians   → 2 midpoints + median segments + line-line-intersection (centroid)
- altitudes → 2 feet + segments + line-line-intersection (orthocentre)
- angle bisectors → 2 bisector lines + line-intersection (incentre)
- ⊥-bisectors → 2 midpoints + perpendicular-lines + line-intersection (circumcentre)
Runs before the shapes + diagonals; He noun patterns are stems (construct form too).
Catalog +5 (all He/En); snapshots + shadow allowlist updated.

special-point-meet.test.ts (5 families He/En, defer, no-theft) + scenarios
diagonals-meet-noun-form (crossing = centre) and medians-meet-centroid-noun-form
(M = (A+B+C)/3). Full 2-D lane green (3451), tsc + build clean.

Closes #44
