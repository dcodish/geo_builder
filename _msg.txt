feat(render+parser): semicircle oriented OUTSIDE/INSIDE a shape (#134)

Operator play-test: "חצי מעגל על צלע AB מחוץ למשולש" errored — the semicircle rule
had no side vocabulary, so the `משולש` leftover tripped SHAPE_LEFTOVER escalation.

Fix (ADR-331): the arc gains bulgeRef + bulgeToward. The parser (semicircleBulge)
detects מחוץ ל…/outside (bulge away, default) or בתוך…/inside (bulge toward),
resolves the reference vertex (a shape vertex not on the diameter side — named run
or the figure's single polygon whose edge is the diameter), and strips the bulge
clause before the diameter/leftover read. The RENDERER flips the arc from↔to so its
apex lands on the far side of the diameter from the reference (outward) or the same
side (inward) — decided at render time, where coordinates exist. Plain semicircles
are byte-unchanged; an unresolvable reference leaves the arc unoriented.
