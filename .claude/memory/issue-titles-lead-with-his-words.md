An issue title's FIRST clause must be the operator's own sentence — «שיפוע הישר שלילי», «M אמצע AB»,
«N על הישר l3». The class name ("a SIGN given about a derived quantity", "a DERIVATION cannot be stated
about an existing point") goes after the dash, never in front of it.

2026-09-21: eight issues filed off one bagrut figure (#1317–#1324). He came back with *"you didnt file an
issue for the negative slope"* — #1323 was open, correctly labelled, and its title did contain
«שיפוע הישר שלילי», but the title OPENED with the abstraction, so it did not read as his report. Three of
the eight had that shape; all three were retitled.

**Why:** he finds work in the queue by scanning titles for the words he typed, not by reading them. A title
that leads with the diagnosis is invisible to that scan, and an invisible issue gets re-reported — the same
cost as [[parked-arms-need-successor-issues]] and [[search-the-prerequisite-not-the-symptom]], reached
through the title instead of the body.

**How to apply:** after `gh issue create`, re-read the title as he would — does the first clause contain a
sentence he actually typed, in Hebrew, in «guillemets»? If the first clause is a mechanism, rewrite it.
Same rule as the play sheet ([[playsheet-is-copy-pasteable-lines]]): his words, verbatim, up front.
